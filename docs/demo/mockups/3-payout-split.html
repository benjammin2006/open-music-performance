What this requires

Automatic splits mean someone is a platform in Stripe's terms: separate charges and transfers across connected accounts. That needs an operator entity to carry it, a money-transmission counsel opinion for the target states, and the parties' consent in the deal memo. Until then the settlement sheet is the product and the venue pays the artist from its own account, as venues do today. Charter section 6.
